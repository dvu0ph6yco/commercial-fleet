The mack truck manufacturer is a purpose-built special purpose vehicle designed for commercial and industrial applications. Applications for the mack truck manufacturer span municipal services, construction sites, industrial facilities and agricultural operations. Latin American buyers increasingly source directly from Chinese factories to bypass import markup layers.
