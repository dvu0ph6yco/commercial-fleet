## Field Notes

Key specifications usually include payload, axle count, suspension type, tire size, brake system, and overall dimensions. For example, a 3-axle tanker semi trailer commonly carries 30 to 45 cubic meters, while a 3-axle mixer setup may carry 8 to 12 cubic meters of concrete. Buyers importing to Africa, the Middle East, Central Asia, or Latin America should also ask whether the trailer can be adapted to local tractors, such as 4x2, 6x4, or heavy-duty off-road combinations.

Before placing an order, the buyer should define the application clearly. A tanker trailer for fuel or liquid transport needs the correct tank material, compartment design, and safety valves. A mixer vehicle must match the required drum capacity, discharge direction, and terrain conditions. A general semi-trailer, such as a skeletal or dry van model, should be matched to container size, axle load, kingpin position, and local road limits.
