One of the biggest mistakes is matching the truck only to capacity, not to actual operating conditions. A buyer may select a larger tanker for better economics, only to find that local bridges, depot entrances, or loading bays cannot handle the axle load or turning radius. Another common error is underestimating product compatibility. A truck used for diesel in one contract may later be expected to handle gasoline, and that changes sealing, valves, and safety requirements.
