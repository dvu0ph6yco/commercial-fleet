## Field Notes

The isuzu 700P rear loader High Compaction (HC) is the latest addition to the lineup, offering high compaction and speed in a lighter body. Perfect for electric vehicle applications, this model is available in 20, 25, and 27 cubic yard versions.

1. Starting the Isuzu 700P rear loader garbage truck. The first step is to turn the key in the ignition to start the engine. Make sure the parking brake is engaged before shifting the gear into drive. 2. Operating the rear loader arm. Once the truck is in position next to the garbage bins, activate the rear loader arm by pressing the appropriate button on the control panel. The arm will extend outwards and grab the garbage bin using the hydraulic mechanism. 3. Lifting the garbage bin. With the bin securely in the grip of the rear loader arm, use the controls to raise the bin off the ground and tip it into the rear hopper of the truck. Ensure that the bin is emptied completely before lowering it back to the ground. 4. Compacting the garbage. Once the bin is emptied, activate the compactor mechanism to compress the garbage in the rear hopper. This will make room for more garbage to be collected and minimize the number of trips needed to the disposal site. 5. Disposing of the garbage. When the rear hopper is full, drive the truck to the designated disposal site and lift the rear hopper to empty the garbage. Ensure that the disposal site is approved for garbage disposal and follow all necessary safety precautions. 6. Cleaning and maintenance. After each use, clean the rear loader arm and hopper to prevent the build-up of debris and odors. Perform regular maintenance on the truck to ensure that all components are in good working order and address any issues promptly to avoid breakdowns during operation.
