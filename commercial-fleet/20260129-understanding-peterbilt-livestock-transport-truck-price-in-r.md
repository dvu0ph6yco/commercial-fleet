**Q: how to repair truck bed**

Repairing a truck bed depends on the damage type and material. For minor dents, cracks, or surface rust, you can often clean the area, sand it down, apply a metal filler or weld patch, then prime and repaint. If the bed is severely rusted, bent, or structurally damaged, replacement panels or a full bed replacement may be more cost-effective. For aluminum beds, use proper aluminum repair methods to avoid weakening the structure.
