## Field Notes

A common mistake buyers make is comparing only payload or tank volume. In practice, axle brand, frame thickness, brake system, tire quality, and weld standard matter more for service life. A lower quote can become expensive if the chassis cracks early or if spare parts are hard to source locally.

For example, a standard 40 ft side wall semi-trailer with 13-ton axles, 10R22.5 tires, and leaf spring suspension often starts around USD 12,500 to 18,000 EXW, while a reinforced flatbed model with better steel, stronger kingpin, and upgraded landing gear can move into the USD 16,000 to 24,000 range. This is why searches such as HOWO flatbed semi trailer price in Nepal or MERCEDES BENZ side wall semi trailer price in Vietnam often return very different numbers even when the trailers appear similar.
