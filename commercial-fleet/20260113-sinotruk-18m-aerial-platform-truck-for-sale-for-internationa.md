## Field Notes

During a factory audit, do not stop at the showroom. Inspect the welding area, machining accuracy, paint line, assembly floor, and finished-vehicle test zone. Check whether technicians follow documented procedures and whether serial numbers are matched to records. If the supplier cannot clearly show how they manage non-conforming parts, the risk of inconsistent production is high.

In practice, the best Chinese manufacturer is not the one with the biggest catalog. It is the one that can prove consistent production, pass a serious factory audit, and supply the right documents for your market. If you are comparing an IVECO aerial platform truck supplier, a FAW aerial platform truck manufacturer in china, or a custom aerial platform truck manufacturer, focus on field durability and lifecycle cost, not only the first invoice.
