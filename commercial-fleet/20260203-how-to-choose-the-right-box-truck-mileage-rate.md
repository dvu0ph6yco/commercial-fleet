## Field Notes

A refrigerated unit is the right choice when temperature control is required. A common search term is **freezer for box truck**, and in practice this refers to a refrigerated body with a cooling unit that can maintain temperatures from about +5°C down to -18°C, depending on the system. These trucks are often built on light-duty and medium-duty chassis from brands such as Isuzu, Foton, Sinotruk, and Dongfeng. By contrast, a dry box body truck is simpler and cheaper, used for parcels, furniture, equipment, and other non-perishable goods.

Another key factor is **box truck pallet capacity**. A 6.2 m box body may fit 10 to 12 standard pallets, while a 7.6 m truck can carry 14 to 18 pallets, depending on internal width and wheel-arch layout. Buyers who ship packaged goods should check pallet dimensions carefully before placing an order, because internal clear space can vary by manufacturer even when the external size looks similar.

## FAQ

**Q: why is it called a boom lift**

It is called a boom lift because the main lifting arm is a long “boom” that extends upward and outward to raise workers and equipment. The boom gives the machine its name, similar to other machinery that uses a boom arm for reach and positioning. Unlike scissors lifts, which move mostly straight up, a boom lift provides greater horizontal outreach and can access hard-to-reach areas such as over obstacles, around structures, or to elevated work zones. This makes boom lifts especially useful in construction, maintenance, utility work, and industrial applications where both height and reach are important.
