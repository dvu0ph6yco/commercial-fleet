## Field Notes

When comparing a refuse collection truck, focus on body structure, compaction method, and chassis ratings rather than only engine brand. A well-built 20cbm rear loader may use 4 to 6 mm wear-resistant floor plates, 3 to 5 mm side panels, and a tailgate with dual-cylinder lifting for safe discharge. The garbage chamber should be fully sealed to prevent leakage, especially for wet organic waste.

Practical field experience shows that many failures come from three avoidable issues. First, buyers select a body too large for the chassis, which reduces braking safety and shortens suspension life. Second, they ignore local waste density; wet household waste puts far more load on the compaction system than dry commercial waste. Third, they overlook after-sales support, especially the availability of wear parts such as seals, hoses, control modules, and tailgate cylinders.
