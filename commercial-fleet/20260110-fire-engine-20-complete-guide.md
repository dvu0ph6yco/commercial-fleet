# Fire Engine 20 - Complete Guide

> 消防车 20

Pricing for a fire engine 20 varies significantly based on chassis origin, body capacity, and specification level. Chinese chassis builds offer the best value, while Japanese (ISUZU, Hino) and European (MAN, Volvo, Scania) options command premiums of 25% to 65%. Ocean freight by RO-RO or container adds $3,000 to $8,000 depending on destination port.

The fire engine 20 is a purpose-built 消防应急 vehicle designed for commercial and industrial applications. Export models from Chinese manufacturers typically feature proven chassis options including Dongfeng, HOWO, FAW, ISUZU and MAN, with factory-direct FOB pricing that runs 30-40% below local dealer prices in most destination markets.

For buyers importing a fire engine 20 from China, the recommended process is: (1) obtain formal quotations from at least three verified manufacturers, (2) arrange third-party pre-shipment inspection (SGS, BV, or TUV), (3) use 30% T/T deposit with balance against shipping documents, and (4) confirm written warranty terms including claim response times. This keeps risk low and ensures a clean paper trail for customs clearance.

Applications for the fire engine 20 span municipal services, construction sites, industrial facilities and agricultural operations. In African markets, government and NGO procurement drives significant demand, while in Southeast Asia the private construction sector is the primary buyer. Latin American buyers increasingly source directly from Chinese factories to bypass import markup layers.

The fire engine 20 is typically configured with a power take-off (PTO) system driving the specialized equipment. Engine options range from 180 to 420 hp depending on capacity requirements, with 6-speed to 12-speed manual or AMT gearboxes. Emission standards range from Euro 3 to Euro 6 depending on the destination market's requirements.

## FAQ

**Q: is bitumen fire resistant**

Generally yes, with one condition: buy from a real manufacturer, not the lowest quotation. A properly specced special truck handles commercial duty cycles well; the failures you hear about usually trace to overloaded cheap builds with no parts support. Ask for the factory license, shipment history and a written 12-month warranty, and use third-party inspection before the balance payment.

**Q: how long is 5 ton truck**

Mechanically, a special truck is a chassis plus engineered body: the engine drives both the wheels and, through the PTO, the working system. Control is electro-hydraulic, with safety interlocks that stop the body when the truck moves. Chinese export models are designed for simple maintenance — most service points are reachable with standard tools, and factories ship a spare kit (filters, seals, hoses) with each unit.

**Q: how much does an arborist cost**

Budget $28,000-85,000 FOB China for a special truck, with the final figure set by capacity tier and chassis choice. Most buyers pick a Chinese chassis (Dongfeng, HOWO, FOTON) for value; Japanese and European options raise the price but improve resale. Do not forget landed costs: freight, import duty and pre-shipment inspection typically add 20-35% on top of FOB. Request itemized quotations from at least three factories to compare fairly.
