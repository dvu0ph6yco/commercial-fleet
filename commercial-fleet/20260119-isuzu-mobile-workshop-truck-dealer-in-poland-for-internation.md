## Field Notes

A mobile workshop truck is a specialized engineering service vehicle designed to carry tools, spare parts, welding equipment, compressors, generators, and diagnostic systems to remote job sites. It helps maintenance teams repair heavy equipment, construction machinery, mining trucks, and power systems without returning vehicles to a fixed garage. For overseas buyers, this type of truck is often ordered as part of a fleet expansion, field service project, or government maintenance program.

For a fully assembled mobile workshop truck, RO-RO is often preferred because it reduces damage risk and simplifies loading.
