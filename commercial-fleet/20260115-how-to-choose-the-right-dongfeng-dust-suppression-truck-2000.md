## Field Notes

For a basic China-built dust suppression truck, FOB prices usually start around USD 28,000 to 45,000 for 8,000 to 12,000-liter units on domestic chassis. Mid-range municipal models with 15,000 to 18,000 liters, better pumps, and heavier suspension often fall between USD 45,000 and 70,000. Premium export builds with larger tanks, imported components, or international chassis can reach USD 75,000 to 120,000 or more.

Price depends on several key factors. Chassis brand is the biggest driver: Chinese domestic chassis are more affordable, while international brands such as FREIGHTLINER and HINO cost more because of higher engine output, stronger axles, and better resale value in some markets. Tank material also matters. Carbon steel tanks are cheaper, while stainless steel or anti-corrosion lining increases cost. Tank size, pump brand, spraying system, engine emission standard, and whether the truck is left-hand drive or right-hand drive all affect the final quote. For buyers searching for RHD dust suppression truck prices, right-hand-drive conversion can add meaningful engineering and compliance cost.
